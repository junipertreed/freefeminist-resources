summary: Published in 1884, this work explains the way the family and the subjugation of women developed in tandem with the state and class based societies. It posits the family and gender as forms of class based oppression. Both a product of its time and a profoundly influential text in feminist economics. Engels applies historical materialist analysis to understand the function of the family as a political, economic, and social institution. He uses "Ancient Society", a work by anthropologist Lewis Henry Morgan, to trace the historical path of family and gender as a way of solidifying power through various stages of human development. The historical perspective is dated, and uses racist terminology.
